# Governance

Lightweight on purpose: the rules exist to keep the project trustworthy, not to add ceremony.

## Roles

- **Maintainers** review contributions, check claims against their sources, and decide what ships. The maintainers are: [@pacocartones](https://github.com/pacocartones).
- **Contributors** are anyone who opens an issue or a pull request.

Only the maintainers listed above hold maintainer roles and repository permissions. Contributing, or appearing in contributor credits, does not grant or offer them.

## How decisions are made

- **Fixes and corrections** are accepted when they meet the bar in [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) and are backed by a source or a test.
- **Scope and structural changes** start as a proposal issue and are decided by the maintainers.
- **Disagreements** are settled with evidence: sources, tests and reproductions. When the evidence is silent or conflicting, the project says so rather than forcing a confident answer.

## Neutrality

No pay-for-placement, no undisclosed affiliation. Any sponsorship would be disclosed prominently and would not change what the project ships. Notable changes and removals are recorded in [CHANGELOG.md](CHANGELOG.md); nothing is quietly rewritten.

## Continuity

Everything needed to build and check the project is in this repository under the [MIT License](LICENSE). Anyone can fork and continue it without special access.
